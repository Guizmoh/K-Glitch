On a phone: Chrome on Android, the FM-1 plugged into the phone with a USB-C cable (LOAF BREAKER has no Bluetooth). Press Connect and allow MIDI. Add this page to the home screen (Chrome's menu, then Install app) and it opens full screen like an app.

Everything here does what the FM-1's own panel does: hold a key or a button as long as you hold your finger, several at once. Drop is SEL + ENV. The screen keeps the phone awake while it is connected.
