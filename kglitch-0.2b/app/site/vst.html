LOAF BREAKER VST

The FM-1's firmware as a plugin: the very same code, breaks, kicks and effects, running in your DAW (VST3) or on its own (Standalone), on Windows. No 240 × 240 screen any more: every page as real menus, a full sequencer, and the FM-1's limits lifted.

Download (Windows) The breaks The manual

The same engine

Not a remake: the FM-1's firmware itself runs inside the plugin. What you build on one sounds the same on the other.

98 breaks, CD quality

The whole library at 44.1 kHz, 16-bit (the FM-1 holds eight, compressed), on an even grid. Click the break's name: every break by category (FLEXOR, CYBERWORM AMENS, HUGE AMEN PACK, CLASSICS, yours).

A playlist

Your breaks in your order, saved with the project: < and > under the break's name, or a key of your own.

Real menus

Every part of a page at once, each setting with the control it needs: a switch, buttons, a list, or a slider with real ends. Long sliders keep a wide OFF zone.

A real sequencer

The break's 32 steps (each its slice's waveform), the kick's 64, the snare, hat and clap; click or paint, the step's HITS, chance, nudge and notes, the 64 patterns above.

Keyboard first

Arrows through the grid, ENTER puts a step, F1–F8 the pages, CTRL shortcuts for the rest. Every key remappable, with its modifiers.

Your FM-1, managed

Read your FM-1 over USB: hear its kicks, send or take kicks, patterns and tracks one by one, the rest of the machine left as it was.

A kick library

As many kicks as you like on the computer, named and filed in folders, heard before they go to the FM-1 or into the plugin.

Skins

LOAF LORD's black and bone by default, the FM-1's 24 skins, one that follows your FM-1, or your own, colour by colour.

Keys

  • SPACE play · F1–F8 the pages · TAB the tabs
  • Arrows: the sequencer's cursor (SHIFT a beat, CTRL a bar)
  • ENTER a step on / off · DEL off
  • ALT+↑↓ hits (the break: its slice) · SHIFT+↑↓ note / pitch
  • [ ] the lane a step earlier / later
  • CTRL+S save · CTRL+Z undo · CTRL+C / V the pattern
  • CTRL+B the breaks · C / V the playlist · CTRL+G the manager
  • QWERTYUI ASDFGHJK the 16 slices · 1–0 and - the pads

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Unzip, copy LOAF BREAKER.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, rescan in your DAW. LOAF BREAKER is FLEXOR's, built on CHOPPAH by Laurens Profittlich (profikid).
