1
A white key held
  • Anywhere but SEQ and ENV (tracks)
  • Its loop: LENGTH (3/32, 3/16 and 3/8 too), PITCH, MODE, DECAY (each repeat fades), each break its own (0.9.71)
  • FX flips to its slice effect: EFFECT, AMOUNT, TONE, LEVEL
  • SEL resets the key
2
A pad held
  • Any page; a pad pressed after the key wins
  • R8 · R16 · R32: RATE (3/8 … 1/64, 3/16 and 3/32 too, triplets), BRK FLT, DRUM FLT, DECAY (what they roll: MIX › ROUTING K4 ROLL)
  • REV: SPEED, -, -, LATCH · FRZ: LENGTH, PITCH, -, LATCH · RND: AMOUNT, PITCH, -, LATCH
  • GAT: RATE, LENGTH, -, LATCH · FLT: TARGET, RESO, SPEED, THRU (BREAK, DRUMS, BOTH) · DUB: TIME, FEEDBACK, TONE, MIX (a DELAY in the chain: its own)
  • HLF: SPEED, M. TEMPO, LATCH · VNL: THRU, LATCH, STOP, SPIN UP
  • KIL: the sequencer's kicks quiet while held (K1 LATCH) · X2 (CUSTOM): the kicks doubled, every odd step of a bar with kicks (K1 LATCH, on at first) · DST: a switch, the kick's DISTO on or off (its amount never touched) · DRV: the same for its DRIVE
  • ARP: EFFECT, AMOUNT, TIME, LATCH; FX while held: THRU, ON / OFF, RATE, SWEEP; several ARP keys (CUSTOM): each its own effect and LATCH
  • SUB: a press, the kick's SUB FX on or off · SDR (0.9.57): SUB DRIVE, the SUB layer through its DRIVE, a press on, the next off
  • KBR (0.9.58): KILL BREAK, the break out while held: K1 MODE FADE / CUT / BAR END (into the reverb) / END FADE (at the loop's end), K2 TIME, K3 REVERB, K4 LATCH
  • CHS (0.9.76): a press, HOME › CHAOS off (its settings kept), the next on
