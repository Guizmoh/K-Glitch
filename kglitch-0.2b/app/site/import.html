Import

Your own snare, hi-hat and clap on the FM-1, in place of its synthesised ones whenever you want them. Drop a sample on each drum, hear it as the FM-1 will play it, and send them over USB. Chrome or Edge, on a computer.

1. The FM-1

Plug the FM-1 in by USB with LOAF BREAKER 0.9.45 or later running, then connect: the page reads the samples already on it, so you can change one and keep the others.

Not connected.

2. Your drums

One short sample each: WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC or anything your browser plays. The silence before the hit and the quiet end are cut, a second at most is kept, and each is brought to the same peak level.

The FM-1 keeps 8 KB for the three samples, compressed 4 to 1 as the breaks. The page plays them at the highest rate that fits: 22.05 kHz (up to about 0.7 s for the three together), else 16 kHz (about 1 s), else 11.025 kHz (about 1.4 s), shortening them only past that. A hi-hat loses the least at a lower rate when it is short: shorten a long one with its Length to keep the rate up.

3. Send

LOAF BREAKER must be stopped while it is written (writing the flash pauses the sound). Sending replaces the three on the FM-1 with these; an empty drum has none.

Then, on the FM-1

  1. SEL + EDIT: the drums; SELECT the snare, the hat or the clap.
  2. Its PRESET (BEGINNER: K1; EXPERT: SHAPE, K3): the last one is your sample, under its name.
  3. On it, TUNE repitches it (±12 semitones), DECAY fades it (FULL: as recorded), TONE darkens it (left) or thins it (right); DRIVE, DIRT, LIFT, ROOM and BEGINNER's FX work as on the others. SAVE keeps it in the drum's memory. Another PRESET gives the synthesised drum back.
