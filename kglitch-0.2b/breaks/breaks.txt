# LOAF BREAKER's breaks (from CHOPPAH's): every WAV in this folder, and which of them go into the firmware.
# ./build.sh turns the lines marked * into the bank built into the firmware (app/bank_data.h, generated), in this
# order: the first is the break at power-on. ALGORITHM steps through them. Swap breaks in or out by moving the *; the
# firmware has 400 KB for breaks: 8 classics at 2 bars fill ~360 KB (./build.sh says if they don't fit;
# tools/prep_breaks.py --list ... --dry-run). A break is at most 106,496 samples (2 bars at 99.4 BPM and up).
#
#   in:    * in the firmware, - not; *= or -= its slices on an even grid (the BREAKS page's EVEN GRID), else on the hits
#   name @X: (optional, after the name) its own loudness instead of -12 LUFS (-16 until 0.9.69) (as heard, 0.9.42; RMS before); @0 its file's own (the peaks to full scale).
#   name NNNNNHz: (optional) its own sample rate instead of 22050 (THINK 114 at 16000 for room, 0.9.37)
#   flexor/'s breaks are all at -12 LUFS (FLEXOR: his DJ library, mastered alike) unless their line says @X
#   file:  relative to this folder   bpm:   the loop's tempo   bars: how many bars go into the firmware
#   (the WAV holds 'of' bars)        name:  on the screen, up to 11 characters
#
# in  file                                        bpm     bars  of  name
#
