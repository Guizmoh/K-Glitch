# LOAF BREAKER (0.9.71, FLEXOR: "assigner des réglages de length de white key pour chaque break afin de mieux chopper,
# que les réglages des white keys restent sur les breaks"): each break its own white keys (LENGTH, PITCH, MODE, DECAY,
# held K1-K4): a break change brings in its own, SAVE keeps them all, a restart finds them on their breaks.
#   app/build/host/loaf-breaker_host tests/scenarios/wkbreak.txt app/build/scenarios
wait 4200
expect brk 0
expect klen0 3
# break 1: key 1 two lengths longer
key w0 down
wait 200
turn K1 2 10
wait 200
key w0 up
wait 200
expect klen0 4
# break 2: its own, as they started; key 1 a length shorter there
turn ALGO 1 10
wait 400
expect brk 1

key w0 down
wait 200
turn K1 -1 10
wait 600
shot wkbreak_len.png
key w0 up
wait 200
expect klen0 10
# back to break 1, then 2: each its own
turn ALGO -1 10
wait 400
expect brk 0
expect klen0 4
turn ALGO 1 10
wait 400
expect brk 1
expect klen0 10
# a third break never touched: as they start
turn ALGO 1 10
wait 400
expect brk 2
expect klen0 3
# SAVE and restart on break 2: its own; then break 1's
turn ALGO -1 10
wait 400
tap SAVE
wait 600
reboot
wait 4200
expect brk 1
expect klen0 10
turn ALGO -1 10
wait 400
expect brk 0
expect klen0 4
