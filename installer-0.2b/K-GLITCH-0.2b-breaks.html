Breaks

The FM-1 plays the breaks built into K-GLITCH, and your own after them on ALGORITHM: 232 KB of room, five to seven breaks of the library's size (a break takes 29 to 47 KB). Hear the breaks of the project's library (FLEXOR's under the track's whole name), or a sample of yours chopped exactly as the FM-1 will play it, add the ones you want to your list, and send it over USB. Chrome or Edge, on a computer.

1. The FM-1

Plug the FM-1 in by USB with K-GLITCH running, then connect: the page reads the breaks of yours already on it.

Your breaks

K-GLITCH must be stopped while it is written: writing the flash pauses the sound. Sending replaces what is on the FM-1 with your list as it is here (an empty list deletes them). Your breaks go in two places of the flash (the two bars: 140 KB and 92 KB); the page picks each one's and rewrites only a place that changes. Remove only takes a break off the list here: the FM-1 keeps it until you send.

2. Listening:

Click a slice, or press 1–8 and Q–I, to hear it alone. Chop plays the slices in a new order every bar, the way the generator rearranges a break. What you hear is the break as the FM-1 holds it.

3. The library

Every break of the project, made exactly as the FM-1 holds them: two bars, levelled, sixteen slices on an even grid, all at the same level, as in the firmware (0.9.70).
